About Opal Luna Labs

Opal Luna Labs is a young company, just getting started. SchoolTasks is our first app — built to solve a simple, everyday problem: keeping track of everything teachers share in WhatsApp groups, so parents and students don't miss a due date.

We're still early, learning, and improving this app step by step based on how real families actually use it. Thank you for being here at the start.

What's Next

We don't have other apps or products to share yet — but we're building toward more. As Opal Luna Labs grows, this space will feature our future apps and ventures. For now, our full focus is on making SchoolTasks genuinely useful for your family.
