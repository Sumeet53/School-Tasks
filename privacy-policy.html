Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information SchoolTasks ("the app"), built by Opal Luna Labs, collects, how it is used, and how you can control it.

Information We Collect

Account information: When you create an account, we collect the email address and password you provide. Your password is handled securely by Firebase Authentication (a Google service) — we never see or store your password directly.

Student information you enter: Names, class/grade, and gender of any student(s) you choose to add.

Task information you enter: Task descriptions, subjects, dates, priority, and who is responsible — either typed directly or pasted by you from a WhatsApp message.

We do not access WhatsApp, or any other app, directly. SchoolTasks has no ability to read your messages automatically. All content only enters the app when you personally copy and paste it in.

How We Use Your Information

Information you provide is used solely to operate the app's core features: showing your task list, syncing it across your own devices when you log in, and organizing tasks by student. We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell or rent your information to any third party.

Where Your Data Is Stored

Your data is stored using Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore for task/student data, Firebase Authentication for login), which runs on Google Cloud infrastructure. Firebase acts as our data processor; Google's own privacy and security practices apply to how they store and protect this infrastructure.

Children's Information

SchoolTasks is designed to be used by parents or guardians to organize their children's school-related tasks. Children are not expected to create their own accounts or use the app directly. Any information about a child is entered by the parent/guardian, who is responsible for the accuracy and appropriateness of that information.

Data Security

Access to your data is restricted using Firebase security rules, so only your own logged-in account can read or write your family's information. However, no method of transmission or storage over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights & Data Deletion

You can delete individual students or tasks at any time directly within the app. To delete your entire account and all associated data, go to Settings → Delete My Account inside the app. Your account along with your account information we will permanently deleted within a reasonable time of your request.

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account remains active, so the app can function. If you delete your account, your associated data is permanently deleted from our systems.

Local Storage & Offline Support

SchoolTasks uses your browser's local storage/caching only to make the app load faster and work with a poor or no internet connection. This is not used for tracking or advertising purposes.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
